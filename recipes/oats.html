oats

Description

Start your day with a warm and nourishing bowl of creamy oats topped with fresh strawberries, chia seeds, and a drizzle of peanut butter.

This wholesome breakfast is rich in fiber, protein, and healthy fats, making it a delicious and energizing way to begin the day.

Simple to prepare and ready in just a few minutes,this oats recipe is perfect for busy mornings or a healthy snack.

Customize it with your favorite fruits, nuts, seeds, or sweeteners to create a bowl that suits your taste.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 2 cups milk or water
  • 1 tablespoon chia seeds
  • 1 tablespoon peanut butter
  • 4-5 fresh strawberries, sliced
  • 1 teaspoon honey or maple syrup (optional)
  • A pinch of cinnamon (optional)

Steps

  1. Add the rolled oats and milk (or water) to a saucepan.
  2. cook over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until the oats become soft and creamy (about 5–7 minutes).
  3. Stir in the chia seeds and cook for another minute.
  4. Transfer the cooked oats to a serving bowl.
  5. Top with sliced strawberries and a drizzle of peanut butter.
  6. Add honey or maple syrup and a pinch of cinnamon if desired.
  7. once prepared reflect on yourself and nature
  8. Serve warm and enjoy your healthy bowl of oats.
