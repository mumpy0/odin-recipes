Air Fried French Fries

Description

Crispy on the outside and fluffy on the inside, these air fried French fries are a healthier alternative to traditional deep-fried fries. They require minimal oil while delivering the same delicious crunch.

Perfect as a snack, side dish, or party appetizer, these fries are easy to prepare and pair well with ketchup, mayonnaise, or your favorite dipping sauce.

Ingredients

  • 2 large potatoes
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder (optional)
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste
  • Dried parsley (optional, for garnish)

Steps

  1. Wash and peel the potatoes if desired, then cut them into evenly sized fries.
  2. Soak the fries in cold water for 20–30 minutes to remove excess starch.
  3. Drain and pat the fries completely dry with a clean towel.
  4. Toss the fries with olive oil, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and black pepper.
  5. Preheat the air fryer to 200°C (390°F).
  6. Arrange the fries in a single layer in the air fryer basket.
  7. Air fry for 15–20 minutes, shaking the basket halfway through for even cooking.
  8. Garnish with dried parsley if desired and serve hot with your favorite dipping sauce.
