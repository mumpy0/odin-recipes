Cottage Cheese Sandwich

Description

A cottage cheese sandwich is a quick, healthy, and satisfying meal packed with protein and fresh vegetables. The creamy cottage cheese pairs perfectly with crisp veggies and toasted bread for a delicious bite.

Perfect for breakfast, lunch, or a light snack, this sandwich is easy to prepare and can be customized with your favorite seasonings, herbs, or sauces to suit your taste.

Ingredients

  • 4 slices of whole wheat bread
  • 100g cottage cheese (paneer), crumbled
  • 1 small tomato, sliced
  • 1 small cucumber, sliced
  • 1 small onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste
  • Mixed herbs or oregano (optional)
  • Green chutney or mayonnaise (optional)

Steps

  1. Crumble the cottage cheese into a bowl and season it with salt, black pepper, and mixed herbs.
  2. Spread butter or your preferred spread on the bread slices.
  3. Layer the cottage cheese evenly over two slices of bread.
  4. Add the tomato, cucumber, and onion slices on top.
  5. Cover with the remaining bread slices.
  6. Toast or grill the sandwich until the bread is golden brown and crispy.
  7. Cut the sandwich in half and serve hot with ketchup or green chutney.
